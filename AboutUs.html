About Branches Association

Dedicated to sustainable community development, human relief, and empowering vulnerable families in Gaza.

Who We Are

Branches for Development and Charity was established to contribute to sustainable community development and improve the quality of life for vulnerable groups through humanitarian and developmental programs.

The association operates based on transparency and community partnership, aiming to empower individuals and create sustainable impact.

Founded & Registered (GA-1306-C) Founded in 2025, officially registered under GA-1306-C, with its main office in Gaza City.
UN Cluster Member (WASH & Education) It is a member of the Education Cluster and WASH Cluster, ensuring professional standards and high-quality services.
2025 Headquartered in Gaza City
